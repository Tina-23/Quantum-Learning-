## About

This repository is a structured portfolio of everything I learn in quantum computing. Each folder corresponds to a course, programme, or event and contains:

- 📄 Certificate (where awarded)
- 📓 Personal notes and study material
- 💻 Completed tutorials, exercises, and notebooks
- 🔬 Projects (where applicable)

The goal is to maintain a transparent, growing record of my quantum education — from foundations through to quantum machine learning, quantum error correction, quantum cryptography, and beyond.
